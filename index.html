    Before you start

    What is Shah Software Solution?

    Shah Software Solution is a Malaysian software development company (SSM 202403307959) that designs and builds websites, mobile apps, business systems and IoT for businesses. Website packages start from RM399 with domain, hosting and SSL included, and seven live demos show its HR, queue, booking, e-commerce and event systems.

    How much does a website cost?

    Landing pages start from RM399, corporate websites from RM899 and e-commerce websites from RM1,299. Every website package includes a domain, 1 year of hosting and SSL.

    How long does it take to build a website?

    A landing page usually takes about 1 week, a corporate website 1 to 2 weeks and an e-commerce website 2 to 3 weeks, once your content is ready. Apps and business systems take longer; the estimator shows a timeline for your project.

    Can my website be in Bahasa Melayu and English?

    Yes. We build websites and systems in Bahasa Melayu, English or both, with a language switch like the one on this page.

    How do payments work?

    You pay in three phases: 30% to start design, 40% when you approve the design and we start building, and 30% before launch. Each phase comes with a list of exactly what you get.

    Can you build a custom system or an IoT project?

    Yes. We build all types of systems: HR, queue, booking, e-commerce, event operations and IoT. Custom systems are priced by quote.

    Do I own the source code?

    Package prices are a licence to use the software for your business. The source code stays the property of Shah Software Solution, so it may not be copied, modified or resold. Your business data stays yours and can be exported. If you want to own the source code, that is priced separately and can be discussed.

    Do you maintain the system after launch?

    Yes. Maintenance and support after launch are quoted separately, based on what your system needs.
