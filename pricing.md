## How to choose a package

### Website: how to choose

Prices differ by how many pages the site has and whether it only shows information or also takes orders, payments or logins. More of that means more to build, test and support.

Only need people to find you and contact you? Landing Page. Need several pages about your company and services? Corporate Website. Want to sell and take payment online? E-Commerce. Need members or staff to log in? Website & System.

- **Landing Page**: good: Lowest price, live in about 1 week; One clear message that drives WhatsApp enquiries. Watch out: Only one page, so little room for detail; No online orders, payments or login.
- **Corporate Website**: good: Room to explain your company, services and team; More pages help you show up on Google. Watch out: Information only, no online sales; Text and photos need to come from you.
- **E-Commerce Website**: good: Sell around the clock with FPX and card payment; Order management to track every sale. Watch out: Product photos and stock must be kept up to date; Payment gateways charge a small fee per sale.
- **Website & System**: good: Login, admin panel and features built around your process; Can grow into a full system later. Watch out: Higher price and a longer build; Needs clear requirements before we start.

### Mobile app: how to choose

Prices differ by how many platforms the app runs on (Android, iPhone or both), how many types of users it has and how many systems it connects to.

Testing an idea with a small group? Basic App. Customers use both Android and iPhone? Cross-platform App. Several user types such as customers, staff and riders, plus integrations? Premium Custom App.

- **Basic App**: good: Lowest-cost way to get your own app; Quick to put in front of real users. Watch out: One platform only, Android or iPhone; Basic features and a standard design.
- **Cross-platform App**: good: One app for both Android and iPhone; Connects to your website or system. Watch out: Costs more than Basic; App store reviews add a few days to each release.
- **Premium Custom App**: good: Built for many user types and heavy use; Full support after launch. Watch out: Highest cost and the longest build; Needs a budget for ongoing maintenance.

### Business system: how to choose

Prices differ by how many processes (modules) the system covers, how many users and branches use it, and whether it needs roles, approvals or links to other software.

One process in one place, like bookings or a queue? Starter System. Several departments such as HR, stock and reports? Business System. Many branches or 50+ staff with roles and approvals? Company-wide System.

- **Starter System**: good: Low cost, live in 1 to 3 weeks; Solves your biggest problem first. Watch out: One workflow only; Each extra module is added later at extra cost.
- **Business System**: good: Modules share one database, so nothing is entered twice; Staff and customer portals with reports. Watch out: Takes 3 to 6 weeks; Staff need a little training to switch over.
- **Company-wide System**: good: Every branch and department on one system; Roles, approvals and an audit trail for control. Watch out: Highest cost, 6 to 12 weeks; Needs one person in charge on your side.

### IoT: how to choose

Prices differ by the number of devices, how many sites they are installed at, and how they are managed and connected. Hardware is priced separately.

Proving an idea with one or a few devices? Prototype. One site with tens of devices and phone alerts? Pilot. Many sites that need device management? Production.

- **Prototype**: good: Test the idea before spending big; Live dashboard from day one. Watch out: A few devices at one site; Not built for a full rollout yet.
- **Pilot**: good: Real use at one site, with alerts to your phone; Shows the savings before a bigger rollout. Watch out: Hardware is priced separately; Covers one site only.
- **Production**: good: Many sites, with device management; Connects to your other systems. Watch out: Highest cost and the longest setup; Needs on-site installation and upkeep.

### UI/UX design: how to choose

Prices differ by how many screens are designed, and whether they are tested with real users and turned into a design system.

Need a few key screens to show investors or a developer? Basic Design. Designing a whole website or app? Pro Design. A large product with a team building it? Complete Suite.

- **Basic Design**: good: Low cost, ready in about a week; See your idea before paying to build it. Watch out: Up to 3 pages only; No user testing.
- **Pro Design**: good: Up to 10 pages with clickable prototypes; Tested with real users before you build. Watch out: Design only, building is separate; Takes 1 to 2 weeks.
- **Complete Suite**: good: A design system keeps every screen consistent; Ready for a team of developers. Watch out: Highest design cost; Only worth it for larger products.
